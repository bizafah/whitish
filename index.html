GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, then click "Place Order". Fill in your name, address and phone number and we will confirm your order.

Are the products suitable for every skin type?

Skin reacts differently to ingredients. Read each product's ingredient list and usage directions, and patch-test before use.

What about delivery and payment?

Delivery area, charges, payment method and order confirmation will be communicated after your order is placed.

What if I have a concern about a product?

Stop using a product if irritation occurs and seek medical advice when needed. Contact our team with your order details so we can review your concern.

PLEASE READ BEFORE BUYING

A little care goes a long way.

Please read product descriptions, ingredients, directions and precautions carefully before buying. Patch-test new products, avoid contact with eyes and discontinue use if irritation occurs. Results vary by skin type and individual. Product information is not medical advice; consult a healthcare professional for specific concerns.
